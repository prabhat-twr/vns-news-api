Banaras Buzz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Banaras Buzz (the "app" and the website) shows local news and event listings for Varanasi, Mirzapur and Jaunpur. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal data.

What the app does on your device

  • It downloads public news and event files from our GitHub repository over HTTPS.
  • It saves the last downloaded copy in the app's private cache so it can show news when you are offline. You can remove this by clearing the app's storage.
  • It loads article images from the websites that publish them. Those websites may see your IP address as part of a normal image request, as with any web page.
  • When you tap a story or event link, it opens in your web browser. The linked website's own privacy policy applies there.

Permissions

The app only requests internet access, which it needs to download news and events.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/prabhat-twr/vns-news-api/issues.
